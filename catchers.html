Catching clinic

Train the details that matter behind the plate.

Catchers impact every pitch, every inning, and every game. This clinic is co-hosted by the Mitchell College Softball and Baseball coaching staffs and is designed for athletes looking to level up their game through focused instruction and personalized coaching.

Participants will work through receiving, blocking, transfer and throwing mechanics, communication, game management, and the habits that help catchers lead from behind the plate. Expect plenty of reps, practical feedback, and instruction that translates directly to game situations.

Meet Your Coaches

Coach Christina Gabriele

Mitchell College Softball

Coach Christina Gabriele joined the Mitchell College softball coaching staff in October 2025 and currently serves as the program's Associate Head Coach and Pitching Coach. Prior to joining the Mariners, she pitched at George Mason University from 2012-2015.

Having spent her playing career working closely with catchers from the circle, Coach Gabriele brings a pitcher's perspective to the position. She helps athletes better understand communication, game management, and pitch-calling while building the confidence and leadership skills needed to guide a game behind the plate.

Coach Kevin Fay

Mitchell College Baseball

Coach Fay begins his first year at Mitchell College as an assistant coach specializing in catcher development. Before joining the Mariners, he played at the Community College of Rhode Island, where he was part of two teams that reached regional conference championships.

After two seasons at CCRI, he joined the Knights' coaching staff in fall 2024 as the team's catching coach. In two seasons, CCRI produced two all-region catchers, including one Gold Glove winner, and the catching staff posted a .976 fielding percentage from 2024-2026.

Coach Fay takes pride in helping catchers become the best backstops they can be and is excited to help the Mariners compete for another GNAC Championship.
