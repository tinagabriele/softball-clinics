Meet Your Coach: Christina Gabriele

Christina is an accomplished pitcher with over a decade of coaching experience and a proven track record of developing elite-level talent. Currently serving as Associate Head Coach at Mitchell College, she brings expert knowledge of pitching mechanics, injury prevention, and the competitive strategies that create winning athletes.

Accomplishments

Gulliver Prep (2009-2012): All-time wins leader (44-4 record), 322 strikeouts, 2-time All-State, 2-time All-American, 2-time Class 2A-4A Pitcher of the Year, 2012 ESPN 100 Watchlist

George Mason University (2012-2015): Pitcher/first baseman, full-scholarship athlete, 2014 season stats: 2.92 ERA, 42 strikeouts, .227 opposing batting average, graduated early Magna Cum Laude

Current: Associate Head Coach, Mitchell College Softball
