What Makes Me Different

A lot of pitching coaches will teach you how to throw a pitch. I teach you how to have a career.

It doesn't matter if you can throw a nasty curveball if your arm isn't healthy — you're not going to last. I approach pitching from the perspective of the whole athlete, not just the immediate gain. Mechanics, yes — but also injury prevention, body awareness, and the habits that keep you on the mound for years, not just one season.

This isn't theoretical for me. I dealt with debilitating back injuries in high school that changed the way I think about training. That experience made me deeply conscious of the importance of taking care of your body — and it's something I build into every session I coach.

I believe there's a life beyond softball for most athletes, and I think it's important to take care of your body on and off the field. My goal isn't just to make you a better pitcher this summer — it's to help you build a foundation that lasts.
