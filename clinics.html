Pitch Perfect Summer Series

My summer clinic lineup — small groups, elite instruction, and a focus on mechanics that last.

🔥 Fastball Fundamentals

Build a Dominant Fastball

Master the foundational pitch that every pitcher needs. This clinic focuses on grip, release mechanics, arm angle, and consistency to develop a fastball that intimidates hitters.

Skill Level: All levels (beginner to advanced)

Focus Areas: Grip, mechanics, velocity, consistency

Duration: 2 hours

Class Size: 6 athletes max

What You'll Learn:

  • Proper grip and hand positioning
  • Arm slot and release mechanics
  • Building velocity safely
  • Consistency and control drills

🌀 Curveball Mastery

Add Movement and Deception

Learn the mechanics of an effective breaking ball. This clinic teaches the curveball grip, arm action, and strategies for using movement to get strikeouts and weak contact.

Skill Level: Intermediate to advanced

Focus Areas: Grip, spin rate, movement, sequencing

Duration: 2 hours

Class Size: 6 athletes max

What You'll Learn:

  • Curveball grip variations
  • Spin mechanics and wrist position
  • Movement and depth
  • When and how to throw effectively

⬆️ Riseball Revolution

Master the Game's Toughest Pitch

The riseball is one of the most feared pitches in softball. This advanced clinic breaks down the mechanics, grip, and arm action needed to throw a rising fastball that hitters can't lay off.

Skill Level: Advanced

Focus Areas: Grip, backspin, arm angle, placement

Duration: 2 hours

Class Size: 6 athletes max

What You'll Learn:

  • Riseball grip and finger position
  • Creating backspin and rise
  • Arm angle and release mechanics
  • Strategic placement and pitch sequencing
